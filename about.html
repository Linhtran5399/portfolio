What drives me?

I design to move the numbers that matter - conversion, retention, engagement - across Fintech, Lending, Crypto and Consumer products. Clearer numbers, not prettier ones: I start from what is stopping people, then design only what removes it.

Curiosity - the kind that doesn’t clock out. I constantly ask why, and I don’t stop until I find an answer worth keeping. I’m drawn to human psychology, philosophy, technology, and the future we’re all quietly building together. Lately, I’ve been deep in the world of AI and automation, exploring how these tools can reshape the way we design and work.

  1. 01

    Frame the problem

    I start with the data to find where people drop off, and name the real problem before sketching.

  2. 02

    Listen to users

    Driver interviews at Be hubs, five traders across three cities, focus groups. I look for the behaviour behind the number.

  3. 03

    Propose, then test

    I bring three options to the team, review them internally, then A/B test the contenders instead of arguing taste.

  4. 04

    Ship and measure

    I stay through launch and the hot-fixes after, and report results in the same metric the problem was framed in.
